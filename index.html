THE SYSTEM

Detect.
Locate.
Alert.

WILDGUARD connects computer vision, edge computing, GPS and long-range communication into one wildlife early-warning workflow.

Designed for the forest–farm interface, with elephant detection as the primary objective and sloth bear detection as a secondary capability.

01 — DETECT

Intelligence at the edge.

A camera feed is processed locally through a Raspberry Pi and YOLO-based computer vision to identify wildlife in monitored areas.
